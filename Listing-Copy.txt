FULL PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
Receipts in one folder. Screenshots in another. Support conversations scattered across emails. Before you contact a company again, bring the important details together.

Complaint Pack helps you explain what happened, what you have already tried, and what you want the company to do next.

Open the HTML file in your browser and work through five steps:

1. Add the facts
Record the company, reference number, issue, impact, and specific resolution you want.

2. Build a timeline
Add events and support conversations. Record dates, channels, and who you spoke to. Link each event to its supporting evidence.

3. Organise the evidence
Add receipts, screenshots, photos, or PDFs. Give each file a useful title and caption. Stable E01, E02, and E03 references make the case easier to follow.

4. Prepare your message
Choose a full complaint, short complaint, or follow-up template. Pick a courteous or direct tone, then edit the wording and copy it into your email.

5. Review and export
Preview the report and message. Choose what to include, check the original attachments, and download a shared ZIP. You can also print the report or use your browser's Save as PDF option.

PLEASE KNOW BEFORE DOWNLOADING
Save a private project to keep editing later. It contains private notes and every attachment, including excluded files; keep it for yourself. Remembered browser drafts are optional and are not a backup. Project files and drafts are not password encrypted.

Shared originals retain their content and metadata. Remove private details before adding them. Manually edited message text is shared exactly as written, even if you later change sharing settings.

PDFs are included as separate attachments. Their pages are not embedded in the report or its printed PDF. The printed report and the message are separate outputs.

The app supports still PNG, JPG, WebP, and PDF: up to 24 files, 12 MB per file, and 30 MB of originals per case. It keeps one active case; save separate projects for different cases. Desktop browsers are the intended environment.

Complaint Pack organises the information you enter. It does not verify evidence, calculate legal deadlines, submit complaints, send reminders, or promise a particular outcome.

FAQ

Is it really free?
Yes. All features are included. There is no paid tier or subscription.

Do I need to install anything?
Extract the ZIP and open index.html in your desktop browser with JavaScript enabled.

Are my files uploaded?
The app makes no network requests. Files are processed in your browser. Sharing is something you do yourself after reviewing the exported pack.

Can I continue later?
Yes. Save a private project and reopen it. You can also opt into remembering one draft in the same browser.

Will it email the company?
No. Copy the message into your email and attach the files or ZIP yourself.

Can I save a PDF?
Use Print / Save PDF and choose your browser's PDF destination if available. The report includes image previews; PDF attachment pages remain separate.

Can I edit the app?
Yes. Its HTML, CSS, and JavaScript are in index.html. The code is supplied under the MIT license.
