QUICK GUIDE
A clear story. All the evidence.
Five small steps
  1. Case details: add the company, issue, and specific outcome you want.
  2. Timeline: record events and contact with the company; link evidence by number.
  3. Evidence: add images or PDFs, explain each file, and choose which ones to include.
  4. Your message: choose a template and tone, then edit the text. Nothing is sent automatically.
  5. Review & export: inspect both previews and your original attachments. Confirm your review, then download the shared ZIP or print the report.
Two different files
Save private project keeps the complete editable case in a JSON file, including private notes and excluded original attachments. Use Open a saved project to restore it. Keep this backup for yourself.
Download shared pack creates a ZIP with the report, message, and selected original attachments. Private notes, private events, and excluded files are left out. Manually edited message text is shared exactly as written; sharing switches do not rewrite it.
Your data and saved drafts
The app makes no network requests. Work stays in the current tab unless you turn on Remember this draft or save a private project. Remembering uses this browser’s local storage database and is not password encrypted. Turning it off removes the saved browser draft after a successful storage operation.
Browser data can be cleared or become unavailable when you move the HTML file, change browser/profile, use private browsing, or clear site data. Keep a project backup. This app keeps one active case; save a project before starting another.
Attachments and printing
Original files are never modified. Shared originals retain their original content and metadata; remove personal details before adding them. Report image previews may be resized to 1,800 px on the longest side and flattened onto white. PDF pages are separate attachments and are not embedded in the report or its printed PDF.
Use your browser’s Print dialog to choose Save as PDF, paper size, margins, and headers/footers. The exported report can also be printed after opening Report.html from the extracted ZIP.
Follow-ups and dates
A follow-up date is a personal planning date. Save a calendar event and import it into your calendar if you want to schedule a reminder there. This app does not send notifications while closed. Requested response dates are chosen by you; the app does not calculate legal deadlines or submit complaints.
Limits
One active case, 80 timeline events, and 24 attachments. Still PNG/JPG/WebP and PDF only, 12 MB per file, 30 MB total original files, and a 50 MB project. Images up to 20 megapixels / 16,000 px per side. Animated images, SVG, HEIC, Office documents, video, and encrypted project files are not supported. PDFs are stored as attachments rather than parsed. Desktop browsers are the intended environment.
Keyboard
Ctrl/⌘ S saves a private project. Esc closes a dialog. All main controls work with Tab and Enter.
